## About the Basic Rules

#### 1

|  Q  | What is an Enekoro?                                                                                                             |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  A  | It's a die that shows the energy needed to use a move. <br>You roll it during battle to use it. |

#### 2

|  Q  | Can I customize the combination of Enekoro's chips however I like? |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Yes, you can.                                      |

#### 3

|  Q  | When assembling the Enekoro, do I need to match the two parts by color? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you don't need to match them.                       |

#### 4

|  Q  | Can I swap out Enekoro's chips during a battle? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot.                 |

#### 5

|  Q  | What should I do if an Enekoro's chip comes off during a battle?                                                                                                              |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | If you can see the face of the chip that came off, reattach it there. <br>If you can't, reattach the chip after performing the Enekoro check. |

#### 6

|  Q  | Due to a move's effect, I need to roll more Enekoro. How should I roll them in this case?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | After rolling 3 Enekoro, select a number of Enekoro from among them equal to the number you want to roll additionally, and roll them again. <br>All the Energy revealed during this process counts as the Energy produced this turn. <br>If you have 4 or more Enekoro, you may add any Enekoro of your choice and roll them all at the same time. |

#### 7

|  Q  | Due to a move's effect, I need to roll less Enekoro. How should I roll them in this case?                                                                                                                |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Before rolling the Enekoro, select the ones you want to remove. <br>Roll the remaining Enekoro, and all the Energy revealed during this process counts as the Energy produced this turn. |

#### 8

|  Q  | Do I reroll Enekoro or Charakoro that have fallen off the table?                                                                                                 |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | If an Enekoro or Charakoro falls in a spot where neither you nor your opponent can see it, only the fallen Enekoro or Charakoro is rolled again. |

#### 9

|  Q  | Can I roll the Enekoro and Charakoro separately?                                                                                                                                          |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Generally, you roll them at the same time. <br>However, if you cannot hold both the Enekoro and Charakoro at the same time, you may roll them separately. |

#### 10

|  Q  | What is a Characard?                                                                                                                                                    |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | It is a card showing the Pokémon you are using, along with its HP and weakness. <br>You place it in the Characard zone during a battle. |

#### 11

|  Q  | If a Charakoro lands diagonally, can I roll it again? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Yes, you can.                         |

#### 12

|  Q  | Can I use a Charakoro for a Pokémon other than the one on the Characard?                                                               |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Please use the Charakoro with the same name as the Pokémon shown on the Characard. |

#### 13

|  Q  | What is a Life Counter?                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | It is an item that represents a Pokémon's HP. <br>You start the battle by setting the number on the Life Counter to match the figure shown on the Characard. |

#### 14

|  Q  | What is a Wazacard?                                                                                                                                                                        |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | It is a card that allows a Pokémon to use a move. <br>The move is successful if you have at least as many Energies as the number shown on the chosen move. |

#### 15

|  Q  | Can I mix and use Wazacards from different Pokémon?                                                                                                 |
| :-: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Only the Pokémon listed under "Pokémon that can use this move" on a Wazacard can use this move. |

#### 16

|  Q  | Can I include multiple copies of the same Wazacard? |
| :-: | :-------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot.                     |

#### 17

|  Q  | Can I start a match while keeping the contents of my Wazacards secret from my opponent?                                                   |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Once both players are ready to begin the match, all Wazacards must be turned face up. |

#### 18

|  Q  | Can I continue to use two different moves alternately every turn? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Yes, you can.                                     |

#### 19

|  Q  | Are there any tips for using moves?                                                                                                                                   |
| :-: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Moves with a low energy cost are more likely to succeed. If your moves aren't landing, try using moves with an energy cost of 1 or 2. |

#### 20

|  Q  | If a move fails, can I still apply the Charakoro effect?                                                                               |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. If a move fails, the Charakoro check is not carried out and the turn ends immediately. |

#### 21

|  Q  | Can I start a match with fewer than four Wazacards (for example, just three)? |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot.                                                                  |

#### 22

|  Q  | Can I "pass" without using a move?                                                                  |
| :-: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>You must choose 1 move to use during your turn. |

#### 23

|  Q  | Can I deal damage to my opponent from my very first turn when I go first?                                                                                                                                |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Yes, you can. <br>Although you can only roll 2 Enekoro during your first turn when going first, you can deal damage provided you have enough Energy to pay for the move. |

#### 24

|  Q  | If I roll more Energy than a move requires, can I switch to a different move? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot.                                               |

#### 25

|  Q  | Can the "Weakness +20" effect be applied to both the damage dealt by a successful move and the damage caused by a "Charakoro" effect?                                               |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>The Weakness effect is calculated after the damage from the move and the damage from the "Charakoro" effect have been combined. |

#### 26

|  Q  | What is healing?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | It is the act of restoring a Pokémon's HP that has been reduced by damage. <br>Whatever an effect states something like "Heal XX HP from this Pokémon.", the Life Counter is restored accordingly. |

#### 27

|  Q  | Can I heal a character beyond the "120 HP" stated on their Characard?                                                          |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>You cannot heal a Pokémon beyond the maximum HP stated on their Characard. |

#### 28

|  Q  | If both players' HP reaches 0 at the same time, who loses? |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | The player who used the move loses.        |

#### 29

|  Q  | Are there any status conditions such as "Poison" or "Paralysis"? |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, there aren't.                                |

#### 30

|  Q  | During a match, can I check my opponent's life counter (remaining HP) or their remaining Wazacards? |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Yes, you can. <br>If you wish to look closely to check, please ask your opponent.      |

#### 31

|  Q  | How many different Energy types are listed for the Enekoros?                                                                              |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | There are 9 types. <br>These are Grass, Fire, Water, Electric, Psychic, Fighting, Dark, Steel and Flying. |

#### 32

|  Q  | How many types of Pokémon are listed on Charakoros?                                                                                                        |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | There are 11 types. <br>These are Grass, Fire, Water, Electric, Psychic, Fighting, Dark, Steel, Dragon, Flying and Normal. |

#### 33

|  Q  | What is "Normal" Energy, as shown on Wazacards?                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  A  | It is Energy that counts regardless of its type. <br>For example, if a Wazacard shows 5 "Normal" Energy, the Energy Check is successful as long as there are 5 Energies in total, regardless of their type. |

#### 34

|  Q  | Can I choose my Charakoro and Enekoro after seeing my opponent's Charakoro?                              |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Charakoros and Enekoros are revealed simultaneously. |

#### 35

|  Q  | Can I customise my Enekoro after seeing my opponent's Charakoro?                                         |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Charakoros and Enekoros are revealed simultaneously. |

#### 36

|  Q  | Can I change my Charakoro during a battle?                                                                                                             |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>Even if it is the same Pokémon, you cannot switch to a Charakoro with a different pose mid-battle. |

#### 37

|  Q  | I would like to add an Enekoro mid-battle to use more Enekoro due to a move's effect. <br>In this case, do I need to show the opponent the Enekoro I wish to add during battle preparation? |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you do not need to show them during battle preparation. <br>When you wish to increase your Enekoro, please show your opponent the additional Enekoro you wish to add.   |

#### 38

|  Q  | I added more Enekoros during the battle to roll more of them, due to a move’s effect. <br>In this case, can I choose to use the added Enekoros even on turns when the effect that increases the number of Enekoros is not in effect? |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>On turns when the effect that increases the number of Enekoros is not in effect, you must roll the 3 Enekoros you showed your opponent during battle preparation.                                |

#### 39

|  Q  | I added more Enekoros during the battle to roll more of them, due to a move’s effect. <br>If the effect that allows me to use more Enekoro takes effect again on a different turn, do I have to use the same Enekoro? |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  A  | No, you do not have to use the same ones. <br>Please show your opponent the Enekoro each time you add them.                                                                                           |

#### 40

|  Q  | Can I choose my Charako and Enekoro types after looking at my opponent's Wazacards?                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :-: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot. <br>You must reveal your Charakoro and Enekoro to each other at the same time while preparing for the battle. <br>Once all preparations for the battle are complete, turn all 4 Wazacards face up and start the battle. |

#### 41

|  Q  | What should I do if the number of Enekoro I can roll becomes 0 due to a move's effect or a similar reason?                                                                                                     |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | Even on a turn where you have 0 Enekoro to roll, select the move you wish to use for that turn and roll only the Charakoro. <br>After the outcome is known, the turn will end. |

#### 42

|  Q  | Due to a Charakoro effect, I would like to swap out my custom chips during a battle. <br>In this case, do I need to show the opponent the custom chips I wish to swap during battle preparation? |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you do not need to show them during battle preparation. <br>When you wish to swap your custom chips, please show your opponent the custom chips you wish to swap.            |

#### 43

|  Q  | Due to a Charakoro effect, I would like to swap out my custom chips during a battle. <br>In this case, is it possible to swap any faces other than the custom chips? |
| :-: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  A  | No, you cannot.                                                                                                                                                      |
